Writing

Deniz writes about the forces shaping business and finance, including economics, risk, regulation, artificial intelligence, innovation and leadership.

Her work has appeared in Connect Magazine and GARP, and she has been quoted by the U.S. Treasury and The Wall Street Journal. Her academic research includes peer-reviewed articles on asset pricing, portfolio choice, hedge funds and investment performance.

She also shares her perspectives through Untangled on Substack and her regular LinkedIn posts.
