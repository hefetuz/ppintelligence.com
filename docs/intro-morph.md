# Kesintisiz Dönüşüm

Branch: `intro/continuous-morph`. Root opens this concept; `?intro=original` keeps the original available.

One continuous metallic letterform: dollar → euro → pound → three concentric coin rings. Equal-distance samples are interpolated directly; no crossfading letters and no particle explosion. Three strokes remain individually continuous throughout.

## Choreography

- 0–0.68s: large sculptural dollar.
- 0.68–1.33s: dollar strokes bend into a euro.
- 1.54–2.2s: euro reshapes into a pound.
- 2.4–3.18s: strokes curl into the milled edge.
- 3.05–3.71s: the coin surface and PP seal finish forming.
- 3.6–4.75s: the same coin moves into the existing hand composition.

Shared hero, accessibility and monotonic coin rotation are preserved. The second exploration pairs this opening with a new platinum sculpture and continuous texture refraction under a broad studio-light reflection. No orbiting particles; see `hand-art-direction.md`.
