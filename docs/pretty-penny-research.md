# Pretty Penny Intelligence: hero direction

Research date: 4 September 2026. This records the evidence used for the hero copy; it is not a complete service catalogue or a claim of client approval.

## Confirmed from the company and founder

- Pretty Penny Intelligence describes itself as a business consulting company, with a promise of saving clients money. Its naming-story post links this to cost reduction, efficiency, productivity, strategy, innovation and connections.
- Deniz's business-launch post explicitly offers consulting, speaking, workshops, training and expert-witness services in business strategy, risk management and economic analysis.
- She identifies strengths in building businesses/departments/teams, business development, M&A, cost reduction, efficiency and technology enablement.
- The launch post names innovative startups, small businesses seeking to scale, de-novo bank applicants, and organisations dealing with regulatory issues as prospective clients.
- Her professional profile identifies strategy/growth, analytics, product/operations, AI/ML/automation/controls, enterprise/operational/financial risk, economic scenario planning and governance/compliance as specialties.
- Recent professional posts discuss AI governance, accountability, responsible innovation and building differentiated businesses.
- Her LinkedIn booking link leads to an active Calendly event called **Introductory Meeting**, lasting **15 minutes**, by phone. The page explains that it covers business needs, business type and timelines. No appointment was booked.

## Positioning used in this prototype

Founder-led strategic advisory connecting business strategy, risk and technology. The site should make a business outcome legible: reduce unnecessary cost and friction, support growth, and help leaders make better decisions. The financial visual is a metaphor for business value, not a claim that the company is a trading app, investment fund or payments product.

Hero headline: **Less friction. More fortune.**

Supporting copy: **Strategy, risk and technology—working together to cut costs, unlock growth and move your business forward.**

Audience line: **For startups, growing businesses and financial institutions.**

CTA: **Let's talk about your business**, linked to the founder's actual introductory meeting. Supporting label: **A 15-minute introductory call with Deniz Tudor.**

These are proposed marketing lines derived from the evidence, not quotations or guarantees. No numeric savings, customer endorsements, logos, success rates or current employment claims have been invented.
