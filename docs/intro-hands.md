# Ellerden Doğan Coin

Branch: `intro/hands-origin`. Root opens this concept; `?intro=original` keeps the original available.

The real hand silhouettes are present from the first moment. Currency material comes from sampled points on those same hands, follows their transformed positions, and travels past the actual finger anchors. Two short particle echoes convey direction without covering the anatomy.

## Choreography

- 0–1.25s: sculptures emerge, with a restrained breath and no warm coin illumination yet.
- 0.4–3.13s: two streams of currency detach locally, travel along the fingers and gather in the gap.
- 1.65–2.25s: the arriving material leaves a readable circular edge.
- 2.02–3.37s: the surface closes, followed by the PP seal; warmth grows on the hands.
- 3.25–4.5s: the minted coin settles to its final size. It never changes vertical position.

The second exploration pairs this opening with a warm alabaster sculpture and living currency engraving: directional lifted bands, fine connecting threads and a small inward reach from the touched hand. The English hero copy, CTA and coin loop are preserved. See `hand-art-direction.md`.
