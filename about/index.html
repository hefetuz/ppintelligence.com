Meet Deniz

Deniz K. Tudor

Executive, advisor, writer and speaker. Connecting business strategy with the realities of risk and change.

Deniz brings more than 20 years of corporate, startup, consulting and academic experience. She has built companies, products, departments and global teams from the ground up, with leadership experience at Moody’s, Bread Financial, Chatham Financial and fintech platform TabaPay.

Her work bridges business and risk, bringing together innovation, practical execution and stakeholder relationships. She works with boards and leadership teams, as well as regulators, auditors, investors, rating agencies and external advisors.
